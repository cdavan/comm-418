## Finding the right full-back

When looking for the best possible full-back position, there are many things to consider. This position is extremely important and dynamic to the formation at each end of the field. From attacking, these players can add exterior layers to an attack or possession. 

Although, at the top level, fitness of running up and down the lines is not what this best 11 is looking for. Full-backs at the top level are expected to engage in the offense and run non-stop, however the first priority is always tracking back and playing defense.

Each of these players play in top leagues like Serie A in Italy, the Premier League in England, and LaLiga in Spain. Every match they line up against the best in the world. 

Pass interceptions demonstrate focus and attention to game details. These interceptions can shut down short passes leading to goals, cross field switches to break down defenses, and long through balls in pockets where counter attacks can damage teams. With the level of top wingers, it is crucial for high interception rates on the wings. 

With a high tackle rating, it shows fearlessness, but also carelessness. The tackles won statistic next to it shows how accurate these full-backs were at not winning the ball or drawing a foul. Fouls and general tackles can often cause chaos on the back defensive line. 

Defenders and midfielders may have to move out of position to support a player who cannot make the important interceptions or win tackles. This out of position formation leads to errors and conceding goals; Therefore, it is crucial the full-back is always sharp for the best.
