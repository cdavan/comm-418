## Critique
While not necessarily a data story, this article by the Washington Post is actively updated with medal information from the current winter olympics. It shows each country participating in the Olympics and how many medals they may have or have not won. 

Something I liked about this article is how clear and easy to read it is. It is easily navigable if I want to quickly jump to a specific country or event. It is very quickly being updated after every event. It can serve as a helpful resource when collecting Olympic data for future projects through the clarity and accuracy of the information.
