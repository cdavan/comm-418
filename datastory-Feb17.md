## About
I really liked this article for many reasons. It includes many different semi interactive visuals like line and dot graphs. The data is clear and very organized. The colors grab the attention of the reader to the point where if you did not read the text portion, you could still understand the story being told through the data visuals. I thought this story was relevant because our climate crisis is worsening as each day goes by. That, to me, is newsworthy and timely.
