## Data story

This story is based on a study done by Guttmacher which looked at abortions have not dropped in numbers since Roe v Wade was overturned in 2022. The story dove into data that was researched, as well as personal anecdotes from interviews, along with other aspects like medication by mail, opposing abortion supporters, and more. It was short and something I did not know, I liked this story.
