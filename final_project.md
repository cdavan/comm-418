# Looking for the best ahead of the 2026 World Cup
By: Colin Davan

With the World Cup right around the corner, the top players from 48 nations will suit up with their national team to battle for the gold historic trophy. 

While many of these players spend their year at clubs in different parts of the world, they are not usually ranked all together. With rankings by position coming within specific club leagues or continents with many leagues, it does not commonly get seen on a global ranking scale.

Breaking down each position into four categories: Forwards, Midfielders, Defenders, Goalkeepers. By looking at the statistics from the 2024-2025 league season, the best players from the great continents (aside from Antarctica) will arise.

## Forwards

When looking for the best forward, a team will need a player who can reliably put the ball in the back of the net. These players are usually not needed for their defense and help generate the offense. However, putting the ball does not always mean directly scoring. Assists are another major way to add to the offense and be an effective forward. 

The way the forward position is utilized can determine team tactics and physical size of a player. With bigger and faster physical forwards, the ball is played down field because these players may lack dribbling and passing skills that a midfielder or winger would have. 

Usually with a through ball or kick over the head of defenders, the large forwards can run onto the ball and essentially just take a couple touches before shooting.

Physically smaller forwards usually carry much stronger technical ability on the ball. As the ball is passed around the field, these forwards get involved in the “build-up play”. This is a much less direct style of play that focuses on movement. 

By passing the ball around, moving into different spaces, and dribbling help make this style of play work. With a larger forward that lacks in passing and dribbling skills, it may be difficult to have offensive success under a coach that operates with these tactics.

Forwards under this system usually create more chances for other players leading to higher assist totals compared to physically larger kick-and-run who will load up on goals, but may not create many assist opportunities. 

Looking at the players on this chart, it is easy to see which forwards play for teams that build-up and kick-and-run to generate offense. Both tactics are proven to work on winning teams, but determining which forward is the best fit for the starting 11 will depend on statistics like these. 

## Midfielders

This position is arguably the most dynamic on the field as it connects the attack with the defense, and transitioning between both. It incorporates offensive skills like dribbling, passing, shooting, and chance creating. 

It also features defensive skills like tackles (taking the ball from defenders one on one), floor and aerial clearances, and formation organization like tracking back on defense to help. 

Looking specifically at ball handling, progressive carries and passes both demonstrate ability to move the ball up and down the field with success.

These statistics not only demonstrate the most successful midfielders of transitioning the ball up the field, but can show what type of midfielder they are. 

There are three main midfielders: Central defensive midfielder, central box-to-box midfielder, and the central attacking midfielder. 

While it is important to have both progressive passing and carrying skills, central attacking midfielders tend to carry the ball more, with sharp passing ability to the forwards. 

Most central defensive midfielders pass the ball at a high rate usually connecting passes with other midfielders or directly to forwards.

Looking at the same list of midfielders in the previous visual in a different visual looking at goals and assists can show these differences in position of midfielder. Players with higher goals and assists tended to have higher progressive carries who typically played in the central attacking role and stood out as the best.

Those who had lower goals and assists, tended to amount to a stark increase of progressive passes compared to the others. These players usually stood as the best central defensive midfielders in this data.

## Defenders

When looking for the best possible defender position, there are many things to consider. This position is extremely important and dynamic to the formation at each end of the field. From attacking, these players can add exterior layers and dimensions to a match. 

However, the first priority is always tracking back and playing defense.

<img width="495" height="450" alt="Screenshot 2026-04-16 at 4 24 48 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/42e6b8e5-be46-4238-92df-c80f2a33a9a3" />

In over 3,100 minutes played in the 2024-25 soccer season, these defenders made an impact on the defensive end of the field. 

Pass interceptions demonstrate focus and attention to game details. These interceptions can shut down short passes leading to goals, cross field switches to break down defenses, and long through balls in pockets where counter attacks can damage teams. 

With a high tackle rating, it shows fearlessness, but also carelessness. The tackles won statistic next to it shows how accurate these full-backs were at winning the ball or not drawing a foul. Fouls and general tackles can often cause chaos on the back defensive line. 

Defenders and midfielders may have to move out of position to support a player who cannot make the important interceptions or win tackles. This out of position formation leads to errors and conceding goals; Therefore, it is crucial the full-back is always sharp for the best.

## Goalkeepers

Finding the best goalkeeper can feature many different skill sets and results. Looking at clean sheets, in which a goalkeeper does not allow a goal might not be as important as a save percentage due to a better defensive force in front of the net.

Using data on shots on target compared next to the saves total can highlight how well a goalkeeper is a shot stopping. While this shot on target data does not include a deflection, that is helpful to note as a goalkeeper could be lined up to save yet the deflection changes the shot entirely. 

Looking at the goals allowed is another factor. High amounts of goals allowed can show mistakes and weaknesses in a goalkeeper's ability with both hands and feet. In a league season with over 30 games played, all four of these goalkeepers maintaining a goal total in the range of high 20s and low 30s is a strong performance compared to the rest of the world's goalkeeper competition.
