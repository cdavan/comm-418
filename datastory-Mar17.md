## Review
This story is about a study done by Lancet which shows a trend in rising physical activity correlating with the warming planet. I like this article because the data and statistics were very clear. It does not include many visuals or charts, but the interviews and anecdotes help make it a story rather than a summary of the research. The study was also very interesting (it is linked in the story). As a fan of climate stories, I really liked this story by the Washington Post.
