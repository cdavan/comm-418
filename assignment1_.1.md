## About the data story
This article has a dataset about overtime decisions in the NFL this past 2025-2026 season. The dataset lists whether teams received the ball first or declined, outcome of the game, date, team, and opponent. I really liked it and found it really interesting after a firery NFL season with plenty of crazy overtime games.
