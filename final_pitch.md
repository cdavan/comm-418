# Possible headline: Creating a superteam with all active players ahead of the World Cup
  For my story, I would like to create the best possible soccer team ahead of the 2026 World Cup using data from the FC26 video game. The game launched in late 2025 and it provides a dataset of all of the players, men and women, who each are rated 0-99 on many different skills such as pace, defending, shooting, dribbling and many more. I would like to use this dataset along with other researched studies about what makes the best possible soccer player at each position to craft the greatest statistical starting lineup with a full bench. The game has a similar mode to this called Ultimate Team, but has certain limits on spending and chemistry, but this story would be different as it would not be made to play other teams. This story would eliminate bias (player cost, soccer club, country, name, fame) and dive specifically at the data and ratings in order to create a superteam. 
  I think this story would be extremely timely with the 2026 World Cup taking place in a couple months, the European leagues reaching their trophy dependent matches, and the MLS season starting back up. I am best suited to write and research this story because I have a strong passion for following global soccer for a long time. This story would be appealing to many different audiences, from gamers, to soccer lovers, to sports data fanatics, it would be super engaging. People would be able to read the story and feel their country get a shoutout or learn about a talented athlete from their country.
