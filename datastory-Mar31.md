# Data story March 31

Link: https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2026/03/31/gas-prices-four-dollars/

## About the story:

This story is about the gas prices on the rise amid the war in Iran and the shipping flow through the Strait of Hormuz. The article uses a variety of different data visuals like an interactive line chart that goes day-by-day from February through March, a color coded map of the United States, and fuel prices per gallon between the different fuels. Within the text, there are statistics that do not really need visualizations. Overall, it was a very educational article and I really liked where and how the data was shown.
